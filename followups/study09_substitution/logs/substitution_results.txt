H4  an argument-light tool absorbs most of the misselection
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Not definitional, and the practically useful result: a catalogue's
  low-arity orienting tool acts as a sink for substitution.
  catalogue     top substitute                share of all misselections
  git           git_status ['repo_path']      122/265 = 46.0%
  filesystem    list_allowed_directories []   339/394 = 86.0%

  Arity is necessary but not sufficient: git_status and git_diff_staged
  both require one argument, and substitution concentrates on the former.
  Among equally argument-light tools, the one that reads as 'orient me'
  wins. Arity predicts the candidate set; semantics picks within it.
